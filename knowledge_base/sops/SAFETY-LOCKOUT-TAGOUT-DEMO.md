### 1. Fundamental Safety Philosophy
Hazardous energy control (Lockout/Tagout) prevents the unexpected energization, start-up, or release of stored energy during machine servicing, inspection, or maintenance. 

### 2. General Principles of Hazardous Energy Control
1. **Notification:** Notify all affected operators and supervisory staff that a machine isolation procedure is commencing.
2. **Orderly Shutdown:** Bring machine to a controlled rest state using established operator controls. Never pull disconnect switches while equipment is actively under cutting load.
3. **Energy Isolation:** Operate all electrical disconnect switches, pneumatic isolation ball valves, and hydraulic shut-off valves.
4. **Padlock & Tag Application:** Attach standardized personal safety padlock and prominent danger warning tag to each isolating device. Each authorized technician must attach their own unique lock.
5. **Stored Energy Dissipation:** Bleed hydraulic accumulators, vent pneumatic reservoirs, allow heated surfaces to cool, and secure counterweights against gravitational fall.
6. **Zero-Energy State Verification:** Attempt to restart the machine using normal operational controls to verify complete zero-energy isolation before entering danger zone.
